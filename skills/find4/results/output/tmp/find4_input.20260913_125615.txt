Topic: Animals

This game is about animals from around the world. Groups should be accessible to all ages — young children, teens, adults, and seniors alike. Use familiar, commonly known animal names.

Suggested group ideas (pick interesting, non-obvious ones):
- Animals by habitat (ocean, desert, jungle, farm)
- Animals by what they eat (herbivores, carnivores)
- Animals by number of legs (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)
- Baby animal names (kitten, puppy, cub, foal, lamb, calf, chick, joey)
- Animals that can fly
- Animals that live in groups (pack, herd, flock, pride, pod)
- Nocturnal animals
- Animals with stripes or spots
- Famous fictional animals (Simba, Dumbo, Nemo, Bambi)
- Animals used in idioms (raining cats and dogs, bull in a china shop)
- Animals that hibernate
- Animals native to Australia
- Animals native to Africa
- Pets vs wild animals
- Animals in the Chinese zodiac
