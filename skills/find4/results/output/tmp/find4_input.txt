Topic: Numbers and Counting — things that come in specific quantities

Each game set must have a DISTINCT theme name — do NOT reuse the same theme across game sets.

Game set 1 theme: "Things That Come In Threes & Fours"
Game set 2 theme: "Things That Come In Twos, Fives & Tens"

Ideas for groups:
- Things that come in 2: twins, binary, eyes, lungs, shoes, socks, wings, hemispheres
- Things that come in 3: traffic light colours, primary colours, sides of a triangle, musketeers, blind mice, wishes in fairy tales, meals per day, strikes in cricket
- Things that come in 4: seasons, suits in a card deck, sides of a square, legs on a dog, wheels on a car, Beatles members, points of a compass, chambers of the heart
- Things that come in 5: fingers on a hand, senses, toes, days in a work week, Olympic rings, vowels in English
- Things that come in 6: sides of a die/cube, legs of an insect, strings on a guitar, faces of a cube, pack of a beer
- Things that come in 7: days of the week, deadly sins, colours of a rainbow, wonders of the world, dwarves in Snow White, notes in a musical scale
- Things that come in 8: planets in solar system, legs of a spider, bits in a byte, sides of a stop sign, tentacles of an octopus
- Things that come in 10: fingers on both hands, pins in bowling, commandments, toes, decades in a century
- Things that come in 12: months, signs of the zodiac, apostles, inches in a foot, eggs in a carton, hours on a clock face
- Things that come in 13: bakers dozen, colonies that became USA, cards in a suit
