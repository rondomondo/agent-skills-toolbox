How to use Find4

Basic Instructions

Find groups of 4 words (or phrases) that share a common theme. Think creatively as the relationships between items could have more than one possable answer. Click the "Library" button for other category examples, create your own or edit and export existing collections. If you get very stuck you can request to auto-solve any group of 4 or even the whole game. Detailed sources, urls and description to allow further research on any items is just an item click away, or click the "Detail" button.

How to Play - Game Mechanics

1

Select Words

Click on 4 words/phrames you think are related

2

Check your choices

Click "Submit" to check if you are right

3

Keep Going

Find all groups to win!

Difficulty Levels

Easy

Clear but broader connections

Medium

More subtle relationships

Complex or cryptic connections

Game Features

4 Lives

You get 4 attempts to find all connections

4 Hints

Use hints wisely when you're stuck! (or God mode...)

Shuffle

Rearrange words to see new patterns

Click to Learn

Click on any solved item to see extra details

Hover to Isolate

Hover over any solved sets to isolate categories

Reset

Click the RESET button to start over

Import/Export Game Content

Export the games

Save the game content in JSON format by clicking the EXPORT button or CTRL + SHIFT + E

Import new games

Drag & Drop a JSON file containing the games anywhere in the page, or double click for files.

Create or Edit

You can CREATE, EDIT, CLONE or DELETE games by clicking the appropriate button.

God mode

Shift then Click to Solve

Holding down the SHIFT when clicking a box will auto solve that single group of 4

Auto Solve the Game

Click the SOLVE buttone, or CTRL + SHIFT + S, and the game will auto solve.

Game Select & Stats

Double click the toolbar, or CTRL + SHIFT + O to toggle extra stats and a dropdown of all games.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Show Extra Detail

Press CTRL + SHIFT + D to show a table of extra category information.

Export

Press CTRL + SHIFT + E to export games to your downloads folder.

Library

Press CTRL + SHIFT + L to see a selection of suggested topics.

Show the Help

Press CTRL + SHIFT + H for this Help.

Auto solve all

Press CTRL + SHIFT + S to autosolve all 4 group connections.

Switch game source

Press CTRL + SHIFT + G to toggle between the Default and Library game sources.
